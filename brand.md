# Brand — Hatch

Status: active

Style: **Live Seal**. A living factory seal. An egg that is also a sealed collectible.

> Sealed until it’s alive. Alive because it’s sealed.

## Palette

- Shell `#F3EBDD` — marketing page, aged eggshell
- Wrap `#E7EEF2` — cold plastic
- Kraft `#C4A574` — tissue, pack envelope
- Seal gold `#C9A227` — primary, hologram sticker
- Ink `#1C1915` — foreground, receipt type
- Pip `#D6B7A0` — the only blush
- Night shelf `#2A2723` — nestcam

Never `#000`. Never yolk yellow (`#FFE*`, `#FFD*`, `#F5C518`).

## Type

Two voices, like a pack.

- **Spine:** Barlow Condensed. `--font-spine`, utility `font-spine`, tight tracking, all caps for `HATCH`, `NEST`, `PIP`, `FLEDGE`.
- **Letter:** Newsreader. `--font-letter`, utility `font-letter`, sentences and catalog copy. Body default.
- **Figures:** Geist Mono, utility `font-mono`.

## States

| Word | Collectible state | What you see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nest | Still in wrap, on the parent shelf | Egg-unit in packing paper under the ticker |
| Pip | Seal breaks | One hairline in the shrinkwrap |
| Hatch | First authentic unit is in the vault | Wrap off the face. Real SKU visible, still boxed |
| Fledge | You can take one | The unit leaving the nestcam, seal broken on purpose |

## Voice

Collector-calm. A little ceremonial. Never hype-bro, never lullaby.

Yes:

- hatched from

- ready to fledge

No:

- baby
- cute
- cracked the market
- moon
- farm-fresh
- unlock your journey

## Rules

Do:

- Keep the egg a product
- Keep the product an egg
- One pip
- Parent ticker always visible
- Real SKU photography after Hatch

Don’t:

- Draw a bird
- Use yolk yellow
- Smash the shell
- Make it look like a farm or a bank
- Decorate with random memes
